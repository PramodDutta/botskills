Set up a new bot for me I can trigger whenever my faceless channel needs a short. Walk me through connecting AITuber at https://mcp.aituber.app and linking my YouTube and TikTok channels inside it so it can publish for me (https://aituber.app). Then configure it: pull topic ideas for my niche, drop the ones close to videos already in my library, expand the best one into a narration script at my target length, check what the run costs against my credit balance, and generate it with the template, voice, image style, caption style, and music I chose. When it finishes, export it and publish with a title, description and tags for YouTube and a separate caption for TikTok. Ask me my niche, length, template, voice, visual and caption style, posting time, and the claims it must never make. Show me the ideas, the script, and the finished video before anything goes out, then save it.
