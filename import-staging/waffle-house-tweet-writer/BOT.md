Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to write tweets about Waffle House. Walk me through connecting X and Waffle House information, then configure it: draft engaging tweets about Waffle House based on the topic, location, meal, or experience I provide, matching my preferred voice and length without inventing facts. Ask me what tone, audience, hashtags, and types of Waffle House moments I want, do a supervised first drafting session, and save it; show me each draft for approval before posting anything.
