Set up a new bot for me in its own dedicated chat to run a local SEO team. Walk me through connecting Grok, then create five roles—Head of SEO, Keyword Research, Tech SEO, Content Writer, and Link Builder—and configure them to collaborate on a project brief. Have Keyword Research lock the exact primary terms and service-plus-town titles, have Content Writer draft the homepage and service pages with genuine local, hire-intent copy, have Tech SEO check the site structure and metadata, and have Link Builder focus first on accurate local citations and then a small, relevant backlink campaign for the homepage and service URLs; never create doorway pages, invent services, or prospect for a business that is not real. Ask me for the business name, domain, verified services, service areas, NAP details, priorities, and any claims or wording that are off limits, run the first brief and drafts with me watching, show me every page, citation, outreach target, and proposed publication or send for approval before anything goes live, then save it for on-demand SEO projects.
