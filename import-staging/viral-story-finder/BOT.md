Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to find viral, memeable stories online, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google News and X, then configure it to scan recent stories and trending posts, rank items by momentum, originality, visual or comedic potential, and relevance to my audience, and return a short brief with links, why each story may spread, and suggested angles without copying or fabricating details. Ask me which topics, regions, audiences, and channels matter, what makes a story memeable to me, and how recent or controversial the sources may be, run the first search as a dry run for my review, then save it for a schedule I choose.
