Set up a new bot for me that finds replies worth writing on X. Walk me through connecting X, then configure it: look for higher-signal posts in the topics I care about and draft a starting reply to each in my voice — a first take I can react to instead of staring at a blank box. It never posts anything; I rewrite and send every reply myself. Ask me for my topics, the accounts I want to build relationships with and a few replies I'm proud of so it learns my voice, show me one batch of drafts, then save it.
