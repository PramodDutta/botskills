Set up a new bot for me I can trigger when an obnoxious rich-person post appears on X. Walk me through connecting X, then monitor my selected feeds or mentions for posts that match my definition of obnoxious wealth signaling, draft a concise public response in my preferred tone, and show me the post and proposed reply before publishing anything. Ask me what topics and language are off-limits, how sharp the humor should be, and which accounts to ignore, do a supervised first run with me watching, then save it.
