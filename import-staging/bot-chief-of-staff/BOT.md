Set up a new bot for me I can trigger whenever I have a new task, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting my specialized Grok Bots, then configure it to classify incoming work, choose the best bot for each job, route the task with the relevant context and deadline, track the result, and surface blockers or tasks that need my decision. Ask me what each bot is responsible for, how to set priorities, which work is urgent, and when to escalate instead of routing automatically, run the first few tasks with me watching, and require my approval before sending messages, publishing anything, spending money, or taking other consequential actions. Then save it as my chief-of-staff workflow.
