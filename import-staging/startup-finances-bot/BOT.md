Set up a new bot for me in its own dedicated chat that I can use to monitor and update my startup finances. Walk me through connecting Nuvio, then configure it to pull my finance data every morning, summarize what changed since the previous check, flag anything that needs my attention, and log income or expenses when I ask. Ask me which changes and financial issues matter most, how I want income and expenses categorized, and what should require confirmation before being logged. Do a supervised first morning review and test entry with me, then save it.
