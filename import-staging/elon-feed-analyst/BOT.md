Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to discuss ideas and brain-storming in the style of Elon Musk, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting X, then configure it to read Elon Musk’s public X feed once a day, summarize new themes and arguments, and use the accumulated summaries as context when I ask for analysis or share an idea; clearly label speculation and never present generated text as Elon Musk’s actual words. Ask me which parts of the feed matter most, how much historical context to retain, and what tone or level of challenge I want, run a supervised first feed review with me, then save it for a daily update.
