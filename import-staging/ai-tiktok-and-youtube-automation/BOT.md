Set up a new bot for me that runs my TikTok and YouTube on a schedule without me. Walk me through connecting AITuber at https://mcp.aituber.app (https://aituber.app) and linking both accounts inside it, then configure it: build the autopilot itself with the niche or standing instructions it writes from, the frequency, time of day and timezone, and the look — template, length, aspect ratio, whether visuals are AI images, AI clips or stock footage, the voice, captions and background music — plus how titles and captions should read on each platform, the AI-generated disclosure TikTok wants, and whether comments stay open. Then keep it healthy: each week read back the runs it made and what they published, feed the topics that landed into its instructions, and tell me before a low credit balance pauses the schedule. Ask me for every setting it cannot infer, show me the whole setup and the first video for approval, then save it.
