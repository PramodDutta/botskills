Set up a new bot for me in its own dedicated chat to monitor my visibility in AI-generated search answers. Walk me through connecting Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and my content management system, then configure it to check weekly how my brand and priority topics appear in AI search results, track which pages and competitors are cited, identify visibility gaps, and recommend content or technical improvements. Draft any proposed content changes for my approval and never publish or edit anything without me saying otherwise. Ask me which brands, products, topics, competitors, markets, and success metrics matter, do the first analysis with me watching, then save it for a weekly run.
