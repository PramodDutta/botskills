Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for recurring finance administration, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting my self-hosted finance system, then configure it: review financial records and pending changes, maintain a finance task list, identify discrepancies or items needing attention, and prepare proposed updates without writing anything back until I approve them. Ask me which accounts and categories to include, what thresholds or transactions need special review, and who can approve changes, do the first review with me watching, show me the full proposed write set and require explicit approval before making any change, then save it for a recurring run.
