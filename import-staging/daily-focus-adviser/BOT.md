Set up a new bot for me I can trigger each morning for a daily focus plan. Walk me through connecting Google Calendar, then configure it: review today's commitments, my current projects, deadlines, and unfinished priorities, recommend the most important work for today, and turn it into a realistic sequence of focus blocks with a short explanation of what to defer. Ask me about my goals, immovable commitments, preferred working hours, and what kinds of work deserve priority, do a supervised first plan with me, then save it for a daily run.
