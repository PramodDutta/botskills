Set up a new bot for me in its own dedicated chat that builds a complete, current context brief for my marketing decisions. Walk me through connecting Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Analytics, HubSpot, and Google Ads, then configure it to collect and organize my company background, products, positioning, customers, audiences, goals, offers, brand voice, past campaigns, performance data, sales feedback, constraints, and active experiments from those sources, flag gaps or conflicting information, and keep the context refreshed from live data on a schedule I choose. Ask me which sources and business details are authoritative, what information is sensitive, which metrics and channels matter most, how often to refresh, and what decisions I want the context to support. Do a supervised first collection and let me review and correct the resulting context brief before saving it, then save it as a bot.
