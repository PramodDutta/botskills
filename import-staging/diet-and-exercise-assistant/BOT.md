Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for personalized diet and exercise guidance, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google Calendar and Fitbit, then configure it: use my goals, schedule, activity data, available equipment, meals, and dietary preferences to suggest practical meal plans and workouts, track my progress, and schedule reminders without presenting medical advice as a diagnosis. Ask me about my goals, allergies, health conditions, dietary restrictions, fitness level, budget, available time, and exercises I need to avoid, do a supervised first-day plan with me, then save it for a daily check-in and weekly review.
