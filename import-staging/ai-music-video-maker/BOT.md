Set up a new bot for me that turns a track into a music video. Walk me through connecting AITuber at https://mcp.aituber.app (https://aituber.app) and linking the channel it posts to. Then configure it: either write and generate the song from my brief, style and lyrics, or use a track I upload; once the song is ready, build the video from it as a new AI image every few seconds, as short AI clips, or as one cover still held for the whole song, in the art direction and image style I set. Quote the song and the video separately before either one runs. Then publish it with a title, description and tags. Ask me about the genre and mood, whether it is instrumental, whose words it sings, the visual direction, and the aspect ratio. Play me the song before it makes any visuals and show me the finished video before it posts, then save it.
