Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for SEO content sync, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Webflow and TryRoki MCP, then configure it: sync blog posts between both systems, improve titles, meta descriptions, slugs, headings, and internal links for target keywords, flag thin or duplicate pages, and show me every proposed SEO edit before anything is published. Ask me my target keywords, service areas, and pages to skip, run one read-only sync with me watching, then save it.
