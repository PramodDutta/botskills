Set up a new bot for me I can trigger whenever I publish new source material or run weekly. Walk me through connecting YouTube, ClickFlow, Google Analytics 4, Ahrefs, and Google Search Console, plus my sales-call, customer-call, and podcast recordings, then configure it: identify the topics and formats that perform best, combine those insights with search rankings, traffic, keyword opportunities, and technical recommendations, and turn them into optimized article drafts in my CMS. Ask me which audiences, topics, keywords, brand rules, content formats, and CMS workflow matter, show me the first content and recommendations in a dry run, and require my approval before anything is published. Then save it.
