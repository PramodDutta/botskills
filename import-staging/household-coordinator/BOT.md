Set up a new bot for me I can trigger each morning for household coordination. Walk me through connecting Google Calendar and Apple Messages, then configure it: check family calendars, events, kids' grades and school schedules, identify follow-ups for teachers and volunteers, and prepare a prioritized list of what needs attention. Draft texts and messages for me to review, but hold every message until I explicitly approve it. Ask me which calendars and schools matter, who may be contacted, what follow-ups are time-sensitive, and what tone to use, do a supervised first review with me, then save it for a daily run.
