Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to complete hands-on development tasks such as clicking a deployment or capturing a screenshot, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting the environments and services I use, then configure it: inspect the requested task, carry out the required interactive action rather than merely describing it, return evidence such as the deployment result or screenshot, and report any blockers. Ask me which environments are safe to use, which deployment targets require extra caution, and what evidence counts as done, do the first task with me watching, show me the exact action and target and get my approval before any deployment or other consequential action, then save it.
