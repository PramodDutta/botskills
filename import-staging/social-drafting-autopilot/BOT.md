Set up a new bot for me in its own dedicated chat that connects the social accounts I already run through PostOnce. Walk me through connecting PostOnce, then configure it to turn my ideas and source material into platform-appropriate social posts, keep my voice consistent, and prepare them as drafts without posting as a person or inventing a founder story. Show me every draft and the target account before anything is published, and wait for my explicit approval before sending or scheduling it. Ask me which accounts and channels to use, what topics and formats I want, my voice and forbidden claims, and how often I want to post, do a supervised first drafting run, then save it for repeat use.
