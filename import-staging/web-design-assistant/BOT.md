Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for website design work, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Figma and Squarespace, then configure it: turn my brief into page structures, wireframes, visual directions, responsive layouts, and implementation-ready design notes, while keeping changes safe and never deleting or publishing anything without my approval. Ask me about my brand, audience, accessibility requirements, preferred references, existing site structure, and what is sacred, do a first page design with me watching, then save it.
