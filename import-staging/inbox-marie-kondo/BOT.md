Set up a new bot for me to be my digital Marie Kondo. Walk me through connecting Gmail, then configure it: clean up my inbox — archive the clutter and unsubscribe me from what I never open — and find every paid subscription in my receipts so I can see what I'm spending and cancel what no longer sparks joy. Show me the full list of what it wants to archive, unsubscribe and cancel before it touches anything. Ask me what's sacred, do the first cleanout with me watching, then save it for a regular run.
