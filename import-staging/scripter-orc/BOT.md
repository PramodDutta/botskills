Set up a new bot for me I can trigger when I need a YouTube video script, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Notion, then configure it: learn my channel, video-making process, audience, performance, voice, and preferred structure from the reference material I provide, and turn a topic or brief into a complete script that sounds natural when spoken and matches my style. Ask me which videos and formats to use as references, what topics and claims need special handling, how long each script should be, and which parts of my voice and process are non-negotiable. Do a supervised first script with me watching, let me revise the voice and format, then save it.
