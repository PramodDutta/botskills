Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to teach laws to young people, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, then configure it: ask for the learner's age, country or jurisdiction, reading level, topics, and preferred examples, explain legal concepts in age-appropriate plain language with current authoritative sources, distinguish general education from legal advice, and save lesson plans and activities to Google Docs. Ask me which laws and situations matter most, do a supervised first lesson with me, then save it.
