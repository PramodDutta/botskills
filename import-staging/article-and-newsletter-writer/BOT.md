Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to write an article or newsletter. Walk me through connecting Google Docs, Gmail, and Squarespace, then configure it: gather my source material, research only when needed, produce a clear draft in my voice with a headline, structure, links, and newsletter subject line, and show me the complete copy before sending or publishing anything. Ask me about my audience, tone, recurring themes, evidence standards, formatting preferences, and words or claims to avoid, do the first draft with me watching, then save it.
