Set up a new bot for me I can trigger to order coffee from Java Moe's or Starbucks. Walk me through connecting my preferred ordering accounts and payment method, then configure it: when I request coffee, ask which shop, drink, size, milk, additions, pickup location, pickup time, and budget, place the order with my saved preferences, and show me the complete order and total for approval before submitting or charging anything. Ask me which drinks and customizations I usually want, which locations to use, what spending limit to enforce, and whether to favor Java Moe's or Starbucks when I do not specify, do a supervised first-order dry run with me, then save it.
