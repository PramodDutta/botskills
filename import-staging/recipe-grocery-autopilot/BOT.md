Set up a new bot for me I can trigger by sending a recipe photo. Walk me through connecting my photo library and Whole Foods delivery, then configure it: read the recipe, identify ingredients and quantities, resolve reasonable package sizes and substitutions, turn the result into a Whole Foods delivery cart, and group ambiguous items for my review. Ask me about dietary restrictions, preferred brands, pantry staples I already have, acceptable substitutions, delivery location, and budget, show me the full cart with quantities and total, and hold the order for my approval before placing it. Do a supervised cart-building trial with one recipe, then save it.
