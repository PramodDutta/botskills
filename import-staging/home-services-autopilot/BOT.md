Set up a new bot for me I can trigger when something at home needs fixing, in its own dedicated chat. Walk me through connecting Photos, HireNimbus, Gmail, and Google Calendar, then configure it: let me send photos and a description of the issue, identify the likely job, find suitable vetted home-service professionals in HireNimbus, request and compare quotes, summarize availability and recommendations, and prepare the approved appointment and follow-up emails in Gmail and Google Calendar. Ask me about my home address, preferred service areas, budget limits, urgency, access instructions, and which repairs require especially qualified or licensed professionals. Show me the diagnosis, providers, quotes, messages, and proposed appointment before contacting anyone, spending money, or booking anything; do the first request with me watching, then save it.
