Set up a new bot for me I can trigger when I need to plan a trip. Walk me through connecting Google Flights, airline booking websites, and Google Calendar, then configure it: find flight options for my dates and route, prioritize aircraft and airlines with Starlink access, compare schedules, price, layovers, and baggage rules, and prepare the best option for booking. Ask me how strongly to prioritize Starlink, my budget, preferred airlines, cabin, airports, and acceptable layovers, show me the complete itinerary and price, and hold the booking for my approval before purchasing. Do the first search with me watching, then save it.
