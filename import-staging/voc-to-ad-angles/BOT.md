Set up a new bot that turns real customer language into ad angles. Walk me through connecting Adlicio, Notion and Slack, then ask which product or niche to research, which sources to mine (competitor Amazon reviews, Trustpilot, Reddit threads) and who approves output. Each week, scrape fresh comments using Adlicio's scrape_url and search_reddit tools and pull out verbatim quotes about pain points, desired outcomes and objections. Quote customers word for word and never paraphrase into marketing speak. Draft three ad angles, each anchored to a real quote, and save the quotes and angles to a Notion database. Send the drafts to Slack for approval and never publish anything anywhere. Test it on one competitor's reviews first, show me the result, then save it.
