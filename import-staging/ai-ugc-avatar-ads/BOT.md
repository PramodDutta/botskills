Set up a new bot for me I can trigger when a product needs fresh creative. Walk me through connecting AITuber at https://mcp.aituber.app (https://aituber.app), then configure it: write ten short scripts for the product across the angles that sell it, and build a talking avatar video for each one. Pick the presenter from the avatar library or from a photo I upload, put the product in the shot as a prop so the pitch matches what people see, give the avatar a voice and speed or lip-sync it to audio I supply, and add the movement notes and captions that suit the platform. Hand the batch back numbered, with the script and the credit cost against each file. When I say which ones won, keep the angle, the presenter and the delivery that worked and write the next ten from those. Ask me about the product, the audience, the promise I am allowed to make, the claims legal will not sign off on, and whether the ads are portrait or landscape. Show me the scripts before it spends credits and the batch before I upload anything, then save it.
