Set up a new bot for me I can trigger for studying technology. Walk me through connecting web search and Google Drive, then configure it: turn a technology topic or source I provide into a structured study plan, explain concepts from beginner to advanced level, collect current references, generate practice questions and projects, and track what I have completed. Ask me which technology, my current skill level, learning goal, available time, and preferred format, do a supervised first study session with me, then save it.
